02 / RESEARCH

Selected work.

Experiments on advice, strategic decisions, and social preferences.

Working papers

01
JOB MARKET PAPER · HUMAN–AI INTERACTION

Strategic Advice on Coordination by AI and Humans

In a preregistered, incentivized coordination experiment (N = 3,502), AI advice influences decisions asymmetrically compared with human advice. Safety-oriented AI advice reduces the selection of payoff-dominant actions more than identical human advice, driven by changed beliefs about others’ actions.

02
COSTLY SIGNALING · SUBMITTED

Help to Signal

Can helping a competitor signal ability? A real-effort lab experiment shows that workers help competitors to signal their abilities and employers recognize the signal. Help can also be reciprocated by the competing worker.

03
WITH ZAHRA MURAD, EMEL ÖZTÜRK & SIGRID SUETENS

Ethnic Salience and Discrimination

Three controlled experiments examine how the salience of ethnicity affects generosity toward ethnic minority and majority participants. Greater ethnic salience is associated with more generous behavior toward minority participants; the evidence points to social desirability bias.

04
WITH SIGRID SUETENS

Other-regarding Ethnic Preferences

Large-scale experiments in Germany and the Netherlands study whether altruism, inequality aversion, spite, and welfare maximization depend on another person’s ethnic background. Overall, the estimated preferences do not.
